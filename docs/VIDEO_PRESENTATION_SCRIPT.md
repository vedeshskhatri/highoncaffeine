## 🎬 Full Spoken Script with Screen Actions

### 0:00 – 0:35 | The Problem: Cold & Helicopter Fuel Logistics
**Screen:** Start at `http://localhost:5173/` (Landing Page). Slowly scroll past the solar hero header.

> **[SPEAKER]:**  
> "At high-altitude Himalayan outposts like Siachen and Daulat Beg Oldie, temperatures plummet to **minus thirty-five degrees Celsius**.
>
> Today, thousands of our soldiers live in thin corrugated tin sheds. To keep warm, they burn millions of litres of kerosene in unvented metal stoves called *bukharis*.
>
> This creates a severe defense crisis:
> 1. **Life Safety**: Deadly carbon monoxide build-up, toxic soot, and hypothermia whenever blizzards freeze fuel lines.
> 2. **Crippling Logistics**: Air-dropping just **one litre of kerosene** by helicopter costs over **₹2,400**. Across 150 forward posts, India spends tens of crores every winter just flying fuel.
>
> Yet the Himalayas receive intense solar irradiance—over **950 Watts per square meter**.
>
> Meet **THERMA**: an area-specific, climate-adaptive generative engineering platform that designs passive solar shelters holding **plus eighteen degrees Celsius** inside with **ZERO active fuel**."
