### 1. The High-Altitude Problem (0:00 – 0:50)

**[ON SCREEN: Start on the browser showing the Landing Page at `http://localhost:5173/`. Keep it clean, scroll gently to show the solar telemetry header and problem overview.]**

> "Hello judges. We are Team HighOnCaffeine, presenting **THERMA** for DRDO Problem Statement 26051.
>
> If you look at our northern borders—places like the Siachen Glacier, Daulat Beg Oldie, and high-altitude posts across Ladakh—temperatures regularly drop below **minus thirty-five degrees Celsius**.
>
> Right now, thousands of our soldiers are living in standard corrugated tin shelters. These tin sheds have virtually zero thermal insulation. The moment night falls, they lose heat almost instantly.
>
> To survive, troops rely on kerosene stoves called *bukharis*. But that introduces two massive problems:
>
> First, **soldier safety**. Burning kerosene in sealed shelters creates thick soot and lethal carbon monoxide hazards. If blizzards freeze the fuel lines, soldiers face immediate hypothermia.
>
> Second, **logistics cost**. There are no roads to many of these posts. Fuel has to be airlifted by Cheetah and Dhruv helicopters. Transporting just **one single litre of kerosene** to Siachen costs over **₹2,400**.
>
> Here’s the crazy paradox: high-altitude cold deserts receive some of the most intense sunlight in the world—over **950 Watts per square meter** through thin mountain air.
>
> The problem isn't a lack of energy—it’s bad building physics. Traditional architectural tools take months of expert consulting to design a single shelter. 
>
> **THERMA** changes that. It's an automated, climate-adaptive generative engineering platform that designs passive solar shelters capable of holding **plus eighteen degrees Celsius** indoors with **ZERO fuel consumption**."

### 3. The Physics Simulation & Multi-Objective Optimization (2:05 – 3:10)

**[ACTION: Click 'Step 2: Simulation' in the top step ribbon]**

> "Now, how do we know this actually works in the real world? That brings us to **Step 2: Thermal Simulation**.
>
> THERMA doesn't just show 3D models—it runs an actual **5R1C finite-difference thermal network**. It calculates hourly conduction through every wall layer, solar heat gain, wind convection, nocturnal sky radiation, and internal body heat from soldiers.
>
> Look at this 24-hour diurnal simulation curve:
> - **The Red Line** is the current military standard—an uninsulated tin shelter. At 4:00 AM, the inside temperature freefalls to **minus eighteen degrees Celsius**. That is extreme hypothermia territory.
> - **The Green Line** is the THERMA passive solar shelter. Even when the outside air drops below minus twenty-five, our indoor temperature stays firmly between **plus seventeen and nineteen degrees Celsius**.
>
> That is a 36-degree thermal lift—achieved with **zero active fuel**."

>
> It evaluates hundreds of envelope permutations, plotting **Thermal Comfort Hours** against **Total Construction Cost in Indian Rupees**.
>
> On this Pareto frontier, the system automatically finds the **Utopia Knee Point**—the exact mathematical configuration that gives you the highest degrees of warmth per rupee invested.
