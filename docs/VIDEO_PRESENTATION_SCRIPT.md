## 🎙️ Spoken Word-for-Word Script (363 Words)

### 1. The Problem & Hook (0:00 – 0:30)
**[SCREEN: Start at `http://localhost:5173/` (Landing Page). Scroll past the solar telemetry.]**

> "At forward defense posts in Siachen and Ladakh, winter temperatures plunge below **minus thirty-five degrees Celsius**.
>
> Thousands of soldiers survive inside uninsulated tin sheds, burning kerosene in *bukharis*. This causes toxic carbon monoxide hazards and costs over **₹2,400 per litre** to airlift by helicopter.
>
> Yet the Himalayas receive intense solar radiation—over **950 Watts per square meter**.
>
> For DRDO Problem Statement 26051, we engineered **THERMA**—an automated generative platform that designs zero-fuel passive solar shelters maintaining **plus eighteen degrees Celsius** indoors."

**[SCREEN: Click into Design Studio: `http://localhost:5173/sites/site_siachen/design` (Step 1).]**

>
> For **Siachen Base Camp**, THERMA automatically configures elevated steel stilts to prevent permafrost thaw, an aerodynamic monoslope roof, an airlock mudroom, and a south-facing Trombe mass wall for nighttime radiant heating."

> - The **Green Line** is THERMA, holding a steady **plus eighteen degrees** with zero fuel."

**[ACTION: Click 'Step 3: Optimization' in top navigation]**
